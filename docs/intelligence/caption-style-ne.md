## Model knowledge (not measured)
- Nepali sellers in Japan write like chat: English product words + romanized Nepali glue (ko lagi, cha/xa, garnus, hola) with loose spelling. Devanagari is used more by sellers in Nepal and for keywords.
- Polite but friendly: dai / didi / sathi haru, hajur in replies; polite imperatives -nus / -nu hola (order garnus, msg garnu hola).
- Openers: "Naya stock aayo", "Japanese lungi available", price first ("¥1,600 matra"), and festival hooks before Dashain/Tihar (koseli = a gift to take home).
- Price plainly with "matra" (only) or "fixed price"; CTA: inbox / msg garnus, comment, call/Viber; Japanese politeness in romaji (onegaishimasu) also appears.
- "Japan bhari delivery", "COD (daibiki) milcha", "stock limited cha", "sold out bhayo", "feri aayo".
- AI tells: Sanskritized formal Devanagari (प्रियजन, उत्तम, उपलब्ध छ), em dashes, perfectly parallel sentences, rhetorical "खोज्दै हुनुहुन्छ?" openers.

## Do
- Write mostly English + romanized Nepali; Devanagari for 1-2 keywords or hashtags (#कोसेली). _[measured (Nepali-audience n=11): body English or romanized, Devanagari only in hashtags]_
- Name the product the way buyers search: "Japanese lungi". _[measured (n=11): "lungi" in almost every caption]_
- Price plainly: "¥1,595 matra". _[measured (n=2): "matra" price wording]_
- One-line CTA: "Messenger ma msg garnus". _[measured (n=2): inbox/DM wording]_
- Dashain/Tihar koseli hooks in season (Oct-Nov). _[measured (n=2): festival hook + model_knowledge]_
- 1-3 emoji (🙏🎁), short lines. _[measured (n=11): emoji rare]_
- Warmth via dai / didi / sathi haru, hajur. _[model_knowledge]_

## Don't
- No formal Sanskritized Devanagari sentences (प्रियजन, उत्तम, उपलब्ध छ). _[measured (n=11): 0 Devanagari body text; v1 NE line is formal]_
- No em dashes, no flag-labelled translation lines. _[measured (n=11): none]_
- Don't claim "original/guaranteed": state facts (100% cotton, price). _[owner rule]_
- No long paragraphs: competitors use 1 line. _[measured (n=11)]_
- Never promise delivery to Nepal or prices in रु: not stated anywhere in our data. _[owner rule]_
- Never width/length or supplier codes. _[owner rule]_
