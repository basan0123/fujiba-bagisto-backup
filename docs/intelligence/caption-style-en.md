## Model knowledge (not measured)
- Small shops on Facebook/Instagram write plain first-person English ("we", "you"), short lines and contractions: it reads like a message, not an ad.
- First line = what it is + why now (code, colour, "back in stock", price), because Facebook folds everything after about three lines behind "See more".
- Price as a number ("¥1,595"); CTA as one verb + one channel ("Message us to order"). "DM for price" annoys buyers.
- Delivery and stock in plain words: "we ship anywhere in Japan", "back in stock", "only 2 left" (only when true).
- 0-3 emoji used as signposts (🌸 👉); Facebook needs few or no hashtags, Instagram/TikTok 3-10.
- Readers spot AI by em dashes, "elevate / timeless / stunning / whether you're", three-adjective chains, ✨ on every line, title-case banners and "Looking for...?" openers.

## Do
- Put code, colour and price in the first line: it is all people see before "See more". _[measured (n=9): product named in the first line + model_knowledge]_
- Keep it short: one idea per line, 2-5 lines on Facebook, 1-2 on TikTok. _[measured (n=9): single short lines]_
- Plain verbs: "message us", "order on Messenger", "we ship anywhere in Japan". _[measured (n=9): CTA/delivery wording + model_knowledge]_
- 0-2 emoji as signposts (🌸 👉), never one per line. _[measured (n=9): emoji are rare]_
- 2-5 hashtags at the end: product word + shop + one Nepali/Burmese keyword. _[measured (n=9): hashtag counts]_
- Only catalog facts: code, colour, "Hosonuno (100% cotton)", ¥1,595. _[owner rule]_
- Sound like a person: "we", "you", one small human aside. _[model_knowledge]_

## Don't
- No em dashes, no " · " chains, no title-case banners framed by emoji. _[measured (n=9): none in human captions; v1 EN block uses them]_
- No praise filler: stunning, timeless, elevate, "perfect for", "rich colours". _[model_knowledge]_
- No "Looking for...?" openers. _[model_knowledge]_
- No 🇯🇵/🇬🇧 translated twins of the same sentence. _[measured contrast with v1]_
- No fancy Unicode fonts (𝒩𝐸𝒲): search and screen readers cannot read them. _[measured (n=9): 0 use them + model_knowledge]_
- Never width/length, supplier codes or "best quality" claims. _[owner rule]_
- Never reuse a competitor sentence (copy guard enforces it). _[owner rule]_
